What this word counter does

Different writing tasks use different measures. Word counts help meet assignment or brief requirements; character counts matter for ads, forms and social posts. Sentence and paragraph counts provide a quick structure check. Reading and speaking times are estimates based on average rates, not promises about an individual reader. Keyword frequencies can reveal repeated wording, but a useful draft depends on meaning and flow as well as totals.

How to use this tool

  1. Read the fields and enter or paste the information you want to review.
  2. Adjust the available options to match the page, text or crawler setup you are working on.
  3. Review the generated counts, preview or report. Check any warning against your actual goal.
  4. Copy, download or apply the result, then verify it on the destination page or in your normal workflow.

Practical tips

  • Choose whether the requirement counts spaces; character limits differ by platform.
  • Use sentence and paragraph totals to notice very dense blocks, then check the actual ideas before splitting them.
  • Reading time is an estimate. Technical language, tables and headings change the pace.
  • Check repeated keywords for accidental overuse, while retaining terms that help readers follow the topic.
  • Your text is saved to local storage on this browser. Clear the field if you are using a shared device.

Interpreting your results

Automated counts are useful for planning and compliance, but conventions differ between editors and assignment rubrics. Numbers, symbols, contractions and hyphenated expressions can be counted differently by separate tools. If a publisher has a strict submission rule, check its stated convention and leave a small margin under the limit. Reading-time estimates also vary: a familiar short email is read faster than a technical guide with unfamiliar terms. Use the estimate to set expectations for a reader, and check the page visually on a phone to make sure paragraphs and headings are comfortable to scan.

Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

Words are groups of letters or numbers separated by whitespace. Contractions and hyphenated terms count as one token.

How is reading time estimated?

Reading time uses a typical adult reading pace and rounds up to a useful minute estimate. Speaking time uses a slower presentation pace.

What counts as a sentence?

A sentence is a sequence ending in sentence punctuation. Abbreviations and unusual punctuation can affect automated counts.

Where is my draft saved?

The draft is stored in local storage on this device and browser. It is not uploaded.

Can I count characters with spaces?

Yes. The display includes character totals both including spaces and excluding whitespace.
