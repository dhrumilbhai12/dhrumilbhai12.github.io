FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Tinkro?

Tinkro is one workspace for documents, forms and async screen or camera recordings. You write a page, drop in a form or a video, share one link, and every answer, upload and recording lands back in that same page.

Is Tinkro free?

Yes. Tinkro is free while in beta. Our free screen recorder and form builder are free to use today with no signup.

Who is Tinkro for?

Freelancers, agencies, small teams, recruiters, educators and product teams who currently juggle a docs tool, a form tool and a video tool to collect information from people.

Does the free screen recorder upload my video?

No. The free screen recorder runs entirely in your browser. Your recording is created on your device and downloaded straight to it; nothing is sent to our servers.

Can people answer a form question with a video?

That's the idea. A Tinkro form can include a video-answer question, so a candidate, client or customer can record their screen or camera instead of typing a long reply.

Can I import from Notion, Typeform or Loom?

Import is on our roadmap for the beta. Tell us which tool you use when you join the waitlist and we'll prioritise it.
