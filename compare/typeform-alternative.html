Compare · Typeform

A free Typeform alternative without response caps.

Typeform pioneered the one-question-at-a-time form. Here's how Tinkro compares if the free plan's limits don't fit.

Typeform makes beautiful conversational forms, and it's the reference for the one-question-at-a-time style. Its free plan is meant for trying the product: it is widely known to include only 10 responses per month, which a single survey can use up in an afternoon.

Our free form builder gives you the same focused style without an account. You export a single HTML file and host it yourself, so there is no response counter at all. In the Tinkro app, forms live inside pages and can ask for video answers.

Choose Typeform if…

  • You need advanced logic, calculations and many integrations today
  • You want hosted analytics dashboards out of the box
  • Your form volume fits within a paid plan you're happy with

Choose Tinkro if…

FAQ

Is there a free Typeform alternative with unlimited responses?

Yes. Tinkro's form builder exports your form as an HTML file you host, so responses are never counted or capped. Tools like Google Forms and Tally also offer generous free plans.

Can I make Typeform-style forms for free?

Yes — Tinkro's builder shows one question at a time with a progress bar, Enter-to-continue and required fields.
