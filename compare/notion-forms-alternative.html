A Notion Forms alternative with video built in.

Notion Forms is handy if you live in Notion. Here's how Tinkro compares for collecting answers and recordings.

Notion added native forms so responses can flow straight into a Notion database. If your team already runs on Notion, that's convenient. Conditional logic for Notion forms is available on paid plans, and Notion doesn't include native screen or camera recording, so most teams still pair it with a separate video tool.

Tinkro is built around that gap: a page can hold your explanation, your form and a recording, and people can answer with a video. You can try the pieces today with our free form builder and free screen recorder.

FAQ

Does Notion have a screen recorder?

Notion doesn't include native screen recording; you can embed videos from other tools. Tinkro includes recording as a core block.

Is Notion form logic free?

Conditional logic in Notion Forms is part of Notion's paid plans. Check Notion's pricing page for the current details.
