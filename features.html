Features

Everything you need to ask, record and answer.

Tinkro combines three tools most teams pay for separately. Here's what's in the beta and what's coming next.

Pages

  • Clean block editor with headings, lists, checklists, tables, images and embeds
  • Slash commands and keyboard shortcuts
  • Share a page with one link — public or invite-only
  • Start from templates for onboarding, hiring, bug reports and feedback

Forms

  • Short text, long text, multiple choice, rating and email questions
  • Video-answer questions: respondents record screen or camera
  • One-question-at-a-time or classic layout
  • Responses collected back into the page that asked

Video

  • Record screen, camera bubble and microphone in the browser
  • Automatic transcript with timestamps
  • Short AI summary at the top of each recording
  • Embed recordings anywhere in a page

Workspace

  • Folders for pages, forms and recordings
  • Comments and mentions on any block
  • Simple roles: owner, editor, viewer
  • Export your content any time

Tinkro is in beta. Some features above are in active development; beta users get them as they ship.
