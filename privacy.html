Privacy policy

Plain language, no surprises. Last updated November 2026.

Free tools: your data stays in your browser

The screen recorder uses your browser's built-in recording features. Screen, camera and microphone streams are processed on your device, and recordings are never uploaded to us. When you click Download, the file is saved straight to your device. If you close the tab without downloading, the recording is gone.

The form builder stores drafts in your browser's local storage on your device. We don't receive your questions or answers. Exported forms send responses only to the endpoint you configure.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist we use your email address only to contact you about Tinkro access and major product news. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not set advertising cookies. If we add privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics in the future, we'll list it here.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and loads the Inter font from Google Fonts. These providers may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site.
