Pricing FAQ

Is Tinkro really free?

Yes. During the beta every feature is free. There's no credit card required to join.

What happens when the beta ends?

We plan to keep a free plan and add a paid Pro plan for heavier use. We'll announce Pro well in advance, and beta users will hear first.

Are the free tools part of the beta?

The screen recorder and form builder are free to use right now with no signup, and run entirely in your browser.
