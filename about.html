About

Why Tinkro exists.

Collecting information from people shouldn't take three tools and five links.

Most work that involves other people follows the same loop: you explain something, you ask for information, and you wait for answers. Today that loop is spread across a docs tool for the explanation, a form tool for the questions, and a video tool for the walkthrough. Each one has its own link, its own login and its own place where answers pile up.

Tinkro puts the whole loop into one page. You write the doc, drop in a form or a recording where it belongs, and the answers come back to the same page — including video answers, with transcripts.

What we believe

  • Fewer tools, less copying. Context should stay next to the answers it produced.
  • Async by default. A two-minute recording often replaces a thirty-minute meeting.
  • Honest and useful free tools. Our screen recorder and form builder work without an account and keep your data in your browser.

Where we are

Tinkro is an early-stage product in beta. We're a small independent team, and we'd rather show you what works than make big claims. If you'd like to help shape it, join the beta.
